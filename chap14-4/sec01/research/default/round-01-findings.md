### Findings

1. **EV Charging Infrastructure Expansion**: South Korea is rapidly expanding its electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure, with plans to assign addresses to 200 new charging stations by August 2023 and a goal to reach 1,800 stations by the first half of 2024 [https://english.seoul.go.kr/addresses-for-ev-charging-stations-in-seoul/].

2. **Market Growth**: The EV and EV charging infrastructure market in South Korea is experiencing significant growth, with an annual growth rate of 19% from 2020 to 2024 [https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/south-korea-electric-vehicles-ev-market].

3. **Production Capacity Increase**: An existing factory for EV chargers, initially producing 10,000 units annually, was expanded in January 2024 to increase production capacity, indicating a strong push to meet growing demand [https://www.me.go.kr/eng/file/readDownloadFile.do].

4. **Technological Leadership**: South Korea remains a leader in OLED technology, although its market share decreased from 89.4% in 2019 to 81.3% in 2022, highlighting competitive pressures from China [https://www.msit.go.kr/eng/bbs/view.do].

5. **Regulatory and Investment Trends**: There is a trend towards increased private investment in EV infrastructure, with major telecommunications and energy companies forming consortia. Additionally, regulations are being updated to mandate higher installation rates of EV chargers in residential complexes by 2024 [file:///D:/GPT_AGENT_2025_BOOK/chap14-4/sec01/tests/refs/ev-charging-korea-2025.md__v_1759368055_296].

The evidence indicates a robust expansion of South Korea's EV charging infrastructure, supported by both public and private sector initiatives. The rapid growth rate of the market and increased production capacity of EV chargers reflect a strong commitment to supporting the burgeoning EV market. Additionally, South Korea's leadership in OLED technology, although slightly diminished, underscores its technological prowess in related sectors.
